## Clusters

### Writing something publishable

Nothing publishable ships without the overlay skills. They are the last pass, not an option.

- Base: /copywriting
- Always on: /copy-editing, /humanizer, /no-ai-slop, /plain-writing

### Building or fixing software

Decide before coding. The upstream skills are cheap, a wrong build is not.

- Base, pick one: /tdd, /codebase-design, /resolving-merge-conflicts
- Always on: /code-review, /git-guardrails

### Interface and visual design

Pick one base skill per job (new build, audit, or visual direction), then stack the modules.

- Base, pick one: /frontend-design, /web-artifacts-builder, /canvas-design

### Making a page or funnel convert

Conversion work without a measurement plan is decoration. Bring the analytics skill along.

- Base, pick one: /cro, /competitor-profiling, /pricing
- Always on: /ab-testing

### Paid advertising and creative

The angle comes before the asset. Never open the creative skill first.

- Base, pick one: /ad-creative, /ads

### Organic search

Audit first, then swap in the one module that matches the hole you found.

- Before producing: /seo-audit
- Base: /seo-content

### Market, customer and competitor research

Name the decision the research has to unblock, or it turns into reading.

- Base, pick one: /customer-research, /competitor-profiling

### Data, analytics and reporting

Define the metric before pulling the number. Most reporting arguments are definition arguments.

- Base, pick one: /dataviz, /xlsx

### Video, image and audio production

Script and shotlist before the timeline. Fixing structure in the edit costs ten times more.

- Base: /video

### Documents and file formats

Format skills are mechanical. The content still comes from the writing cluster.

- Base, pick one: /pptx, /docx, /pdf

### Deciding and de-risking

Run this before the work, not after. One question at a time beats a list of options.

- Base: /grilling

### Sales, outreach and closing

The list is upstream of the message. A perfect email to the wrong person is still a miss.

- Base, pick one: /cold-email, /prospecting

### Social platforms and community

Same message, different platform grammar. Repurposing is a rewrite, not a copy-paste.

- Base: /social

### Memory, notes and corpora

Query the map before reading the territory. It is what stops you re-reading everything.

- Base: /memory-search

### Skills and agent configuration

Changing the harness is a decision too. Back up settings before writing to them.

- Base, pick one: /skill-creator, /find-skills

## Unplaced (3)
